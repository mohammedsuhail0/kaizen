What We Offer

Programs & Classes

From Nursery to Class 10—one complete journey of English medium education with modern teaching and Islamic values at NRCHS.

New Rosary Convent High School offers classes from Nursery (NUR) through Class 10. Every stage is designed to build a strong foundation in academics, language, and character. We follow an English medium curriculum and integrate Islamic education and values throughout, so students are prepared for both board examinations and life ahead.

NUR

Nursery

Our youngest learners begin their journey in a safe, playful environment. We focus on early literacy, numbers, social skills, and creative activities. Islamic values and simple duas are introduced in an age-appropriate way so children feel at home from day one.

LKG

Lower Kindergarten

LKG builds on the Nursery foundation with more structured learning. Children develop reading and writing readiness, basic maths, and good habits. We continue to nurture both academic readiness and moral values in a caring, disciplined setting.

UKG

Upper Kindergarten

In UKG, students strengthen their readiness for primary school. Emphasis is on English, mathematics, and general awareness, along with Islamic studies and character development. By the end of UKG, children are well prepared for Class 1.

01 – 05

Primary (Classes 1 – 5)

Primary years at NRCHS lay a strong academic and values base. Students study English, Mathematics, Science, Social Studies, and Islamic education. We use modern teaching methods and encourage curiosity, discipline, and respect. The goal is to build confident, capable learners who are ready for middle school.

06 – 08

Middle School (Classes 6 – 8)

Middle school focuses on deeper subject knowledge and critical thinking. The curriculum is designed to prepare students for secondary education while keeping Islamic values and ethics at the centre. We support every student with attention to both academics and character.

09 – 10

Secondary (Classes 9 – 10)

Classes 9 and 10 prepare students for board examinations and beyond. We offer rigorous teaching in all subjects, revision support, and guidance so that every student can perform to the best of their ability. Islamic education and values continue to be part of school life, helping students step into the next phase of life as responsible, well-rounded individuals.
