Our Story

About NRCHS

New Rosary Convent High School — where modern education and Islamic values come together in Riyasth Nagar, Hyderabad.

Our Motto

"The best English medium school with modern and Islamic education."

This motto guides everything we do. We aim to give every student a strong academic foundation in English, modern teaching methods, and a values-based education rooted in Islamic principles—so they grow as confident, capable, and responsible individuals.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide quality English medium education in a safe, caring environment. We combine a modern curriculum with Islamic education so that students excel in their studies and grow in character, discipline, and faith. We believe every child deserves the opportunity to learn, dream, and succeed.

Our Vision

We envision NRCHS as a school that families in Riyasth Nagar and beyond trust—a place known for academic excellence, strong values, and a welcoming community. We want our students to leave not only prepared for higher education and the world of work, but also as responsible, ethical members of society.

Our Founders

New Rosary Convent High School was founded by two brothers—Qmar and Hafez. Their vision was to create a school that offers the best of both worlds: excellent English medium education and a strong grounding in Islamic values. Together, they laid the foundation for a school where children from Nursery to Class 10 could learn, grow, and thrive in a supportive and disciplined environment.

Today, NRCHS continues to stand on that vision—serving hundreds of students and families in Riyasth Nagar, Hyderabad, with the same commitment to quality and values.

What We Stand For

Quality

High standards in teaching, curriculum, and student support so every child can achieve their best.

Values

Integrity, respect, and responsibility woven into daily school life and Islamic education.

Community

A close-knit, inclusive school community where parents, teachers, and students work together.
